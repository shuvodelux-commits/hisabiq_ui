### D08.4 Recommended identity, subledger and control-account model

**Verdict: one Party plus versioned relationship rows, source-owned obligations and journal-line attribution.** Flags alone cannot carry lifecycle/version/permission history. Separate person tables duplicate identities. A universal obligation engine would duplicate mature trade/expense modules. The hybrid keeps existing deep modules and adds only loan and owner-specific facts.

| Object | Proposed responsibilities and cardinality |
|---|---|
| Party | Existing tenant-owned identity ULID; names/contact details are not keys for automatic merges. Reuse the same partyId across roles. Search/duplicate warnings help selection; ambiguous existing duplicates require reviewed mapping, not silent identity inference. |
| Relationship | One durable row per `(tenant, party, kind)` for all seven kinds, with ULID, ACTIVE/INACTIVE, version, created/updated actor/time. Revival keeps the ID. Existing `party_roles` remains the three-role compatibility source during transition; there must be one authoritative membership writer and no independently editable duplicate flags. |
| Trading subledger | CUSTOMER: AR plus separate CUSTOMER_ADVANCE; SUPPLIER: AP plus SUPPLIER_ADVANCE; VENDOR: consignment VENDOR_PAYABLE plus VENDOR_ADVANCE. Sales/purchase/vendor source IDs and allocations remain authoritative. Multiple invoices/terms attach to one relationship, not duplicate parties or memberships. |
| Loan agreement | ULID, relationshipId (LENDER or BORROWER), agreement number, direction, BDT currency, agreementDate, nullable dueDate, ACTIVE/CLOSED, version, optional reference/note, lender legacy accountId when applicable. Many agreements per relationship; direction/owner immutable after posting. A signed agreement with no funded amount creates no journal. |
| Principal origination and allocation | Opening or received/lent source establishes principal, not an editable balance field. Repayment/collection allocations reference original principal-bearing lines inside the selected agreement. Each settlement is its own source; partial allocations and reversals preserve history. |
| Owner subledger | One OWNER relationship, with separately attributed capital, drawings, returned drawings and explicitly unresolved legacy current-account items. No ownership percentages, profit allocations, fixed-capital partnership system or share register is invented. |
| Payee | Persistent selection/classification of an identity, not an independent debit/credit ledger. Expense activity and genuine D07 due amounts are source projections. Do not duplicate a supplier or employee merely to make them selectable. No Payee flag creates debt. |
| Journal attribution | One owning relationship per relevant journal line, optionally one loan agreement; validated component and source lineage. Shared controls have many owners through these dimensions. Counterpart money/Opening Equity lines must not enter relationship running balances. |

Deactivation is a versioned master-data transition, not financial deletion. An inactive Party/relationship blocks new obligations, new loans, fresh capital/drawings and new openings; it still permits separately authorized reading, settlement/refund of existing obligations, RETURN_DRAWING and source correction. All historical lines and nonzero gross balances remain in statements, overdue lists and control reconciliation regardless of active status. Deactivation never forgives debt or removes amounts through an active-only join; source permission and money/correction guards still apply. Physical identity merging/reassignment with postings is a separate reviewed procedure, not a deactivation shortcut.

**Chart decision proposed for approval:** retain existing lender `LOAN:{accountId}` LIABILITY code and ID. Add metadata meaning `LENDER_PRINCIPAL_PAYABLE`, non-money, agreement-linked; do not rewrite persisted codes, create a new lender family or equate accountId with agreementId. ### D08.6 Source ownership, loan lifecycle, owner treatment and openings

**Loan lifecycle.** Creation is master data only; principal begins at a historical opening, real loan receipt or real lending event. One agreement has one direction. Multiple loans with the same party use distinct agreement IDs/numbers, not new Party identities. A receipt/disbursement targets one selected agreement; a repayment/collection allocates within that agreement to explicitly selected origination lines or deterministic oldest-first `(businessDate, createdAt, id)`, returned in the response. Do not allocate by party-wide balance, silently select the newest loan, or settle a Customer/Supplier balance. Context can supply the agreement; if several exist and no selection is provided, return a selection-required error. The current dialog has no agreement field: this is a backend contract plus later minimal contextual selection gap, not permission to delete the dialog or guess a loan. New borrowing may create an agreement and its first receipt atomically through the same owner; a mere “Lender Credit” label cannot decide between a new loan and a top-up without explicit context.

Partial and early repayments up to funded outstanding are valid, even before dueDate; dueDate is a reminder/maturity fact, never an automatic posting. AgreementDate cannot follow an origination; a specified dueDate cannot precede agreementDate. Origination and settlement dates cannot be in the future. A repayment cannot allocate to a later-dated origination, and a backdated operation/correction must leave nonnegative principal at every affected effective business-date cutoff, not merely today. Funding more principal is a new origination under the selected approved agreement. A fully unfunded/repaid agreement can close only at zero principal with no pending source dependency; closing never writes off debt. Reopen is versioned and audited. Over-repayment or over-collection is rejected atomically; no negative loan, automatic opposite loan, interest allocation or generic advance. A genuine excess held for later refund needs a separately approved advance source and chart, not a principal sign flip. Reversing an origination/opening must not leave allocations orphaned or make outstanding negative; unwind/reallocate dependent sources with impact review through their owner before reduction, never silently delete repayments. A real principal refund/disbursement is a new money event, distinguishable from undoing an erroneous record.

**Owner treatment.** Historical capital is J09 against OE; today's genuine investment is J14 against money. Nonrepayable drawings reduce equity J15, not profit. RETURN_DRAWING J16 is linked restitution, capped at the original unreturned drawing; a new investment credits CAP instead. A drawing with effective return descendants cannot be voided or reduced below the returned amount; lock drawing/return lineages and require source resolution first. A valid drawing correction preserves or atomically rebases those links to its replacement version without posting the returned cash again. Owner lending to the shop or taking a recoverable business loan uses the same Party's LENDER/BORROWER agreement and controls. Never infer these from a sign, memo, Owner label or bank deposit. Capital/drawings remain separate per owner; one owner's debit never hides another's credit. A drawing may exceed contributed capital where legitimately funded by retained profit; an arbitrary capital-only cap is not imposed, but overdraft and legal distribution rules still apply. Negative equity is not automatically converted to borrower receivable. No automatic profit sharing, drawings closing journal, loan forgiveness, owner-funded expense reimbursement, capital conversion or corporate distribution is authorized by D08; those require the appropriate actual source and classified terms.

**Interest boundary.** Recommend D08 principal operations only. Reject an interest field on a principal-only request rather than ignore it. Paid lender interest uses a real validated expense category/source, with optional loan reference that does not affect principal. Accrued/unpaid/partial interest must follow D07 recognition/settlement; already-accrued interest payments debit its payable, not expense again. Borrower interest recognition/collection, rates, compounding, schedules, fees, withholding and capitalization remain blocked for a separately approved contract; never use SALES, principal or OWNER capital as a substitute. An interest-bearing agreement cannot be presented as a complete accrual financial position until interest and any required measurement adjustment are accounted for. Combined principal+interest tender, if later approved, needs owning-service `*Within(tx, ...)` composition and exact leg allocations in one UOW; two independent HTTP submissions are not atomic.

**Payee/vendor boundary.** PAYEE is recipient capability only. D07 owns EXPENSE_PAYABLE and later settlements, including mandatory canonical creditor identity for unpaid amounts; fully paid miscellaneous expense may retain optional payee under its existing contract. A supplier's rent invoice posted as expense has an expense obligation, not a second purchase bill for the same cost. VENDOR_PAYABLE is for existing consignment/deemed-purchase source ownership, not every person commonly called a vendor. A vendor purchase bill uses SUPPLIER AP; goods sold to that vendor use CUSTOMER AR and the existing delegated sales/receipt service. A convenience Vendor workspace can link to those sources only with the other role's permission; it never counts their amounts again in Vendor totals. D07—not D08 and not MOD-007—owns expense recognition/settlement; its module is MOD-012. Historical Payee debt needs an identified expense-opening source, never a bare PAYEE opening.

**Opening lineage and correction.** Openings use a cutover/as-of balance and evidence reference; they are not replays of lifetime cash flows. No opening request accepts money legs/session fields. Initial zero omits a posting; zero correction reverses only. Uniqueness is per `(tenant, relationship, component, agreement-or-none, cutover)` lineage, with at most one effective starting position per component/obligation; cutover is frozen for the adopted books, not a way to reopen every year. CUSTOMER AR and CUSTOMER_ADVANCE can coexist, as can supplier/vendor due and advance, without netting. MOD-004's legacy one-opening-per-role contract needs additive per-component support before claiming gross opening parity; never post an opposite second opening through an unrelated writer.

Lender accounts already opened by ManageAccountsService must be **linked, not reopened**. The linking command proves original ACCOUNT_OPENING ID, subsequent journal history and amount/date; adopts that source into the agreement's opening lineage and immutable attribution mapping, with no journal/money/Opening Equity change. If it has no opening, create+link+open in one UOW only. Concurrent account/party opening attempts lock the same account/agreement and uniqueness key. Once linked, the legacy account-opening editor must delegate to that same lineage owner or reject with an actionable reference; it cannot independently change principal or create a duplicate source. Customer/supplier detailed imported invoices and a lump-sum opening cannot represent the same debt twice.

For new D08 openings, corrections require expectedVersion, reason, idempotency, unchanged owner/direction, original and target open periods, and capacity checks against allocations. Reverse the effective prior version and repost its replacement, append distinct correction/reversal event IDs; no journal deletion. Whenever an origin is replaced, append compensating allocation events that release its prior-line allocations and reattach the valid settlements to the replacement origination line in the same UOW, checking their historical dates and capacity. An increase also needs this transfer; a reduction is valid only when all retained allocations still fit. This allocation transfer has no new cash/expense/principal journal effect beyond the opening/origination correction itself. If valid reallocation is impossible, reject the correction and require separately authorized source resolution. A no-op requires unchanged amount, date and classification; a same-amount date correction still reverse/reposts at the appropriate dates. An audited note-only update stays source metadata. Closed historical opening correction is blocked by default; a separately authorized period-unlock or approved prior-period adjustment workflow is needed, without reopening closed books silently. Preserve existing B2 strict opening guard and B4 delta legacy behavior until their separately approved adaptation; do not pretend they already implement this new rule. B7 existing money corrections may place both reversal/repost in the current open period with originalBusinessDate and reason; reuse its owner contract after MOD-005 provenance review. Closed sessions/physical counts are immutable. An accounting correction is not proof that physical money moved now.

**Opening Equity is conversion clearing, not income or a permanent plug.** Reconcile a complete opening worksheet including assets, liabilities, capital/drawings and retained results. Capital J09 reclassifies existing opening equity and does not import historical cash again. A remaining OE balance may be valid while conversion is incomplete, but is explicitly unreconciled; do not zero it to sales/expense or claim a completed Balance Sheet without classification. No automatic retained-earnings account addition or balancing journal is approved here.

```ts
type LoanAction = LoanCommon & (
  { kind: 'LOAN_RECEIVED'|'MONEY_LENT'; allocation?: never } |
  { kind: 'LOAN_REPAYMENT'|'BORROWER_COLLECTION'; allocation: PrincipalAllocation }
);
type OwnerAction = {
  kind: 'CAPITAL_CONTRIBUTION'|'DRAWING'|'RETURN_DRAWING';
  partyId: ULID; relationshipId: ULID; expectedRelationshipVersion: PositiveInt;
  amountMinor: SafePositiveInt; businessDate: LocalDate; paymentLegs: MoneyLeg[];
  originalDrawingSourceId?: ULID; expectedDrawingVersion?: PositiveInt;
  reference: string|null; note: string|null; operationId: ULID;
};
type OpeningRequest = {
  partyId: ULID; relationshipId: ULID; agreementId?: ULID;
  component: 'TRADE_DUE'|'TRADE_ADVANCE'|'LOAN_PRINCIPAL'|'OWNER_CAPITAL';
  amountMinor: SafePositiveInt; businessDate: LocalDate; evidenceReference: string;
  note: string|null; expectedRelationshipVersion: PositiveInt; expectedAgreementVersion?: PositiveInt;
  operationId: ULID;
};
type OpeningCorrection = {
  expectedVersion: PositiveInt; replacementAmountMinor: SafeNonnegativeInt;
  businessDate: LocalDate; reason: NonemptyString; operationId: ULID;
};
```

These are discriminated shapes with conditional validation, not permissive combinations: receive/repay requires LENDER; lend/collect requires BORROWER; owner commands require OWNER. Origination kinds forbid allocation; repayments/collections require it, with unique explicit origin IDs and exact allocated sum equal amountMinor. RETURN_DRAWING alone requires originalDrawingSourceId and expectedDrawingVersion; other owner kinds forbid them. Openings require agreement/version for loan principal and forbid agreement for owner/trade; TRADE_ADVANCE requires an actual approved advance component, not arbitrary reverse-sign loan. RelationshipId must resolve to partyId and requested kind. Unique nonempty legs, verified method/account match, exact sum equal amountMinor, positive safe amounts and no LOAN/non-money tender are mandatory. No principal request accepts interest/discount/write-off or arbitrary debit/credit sides. Proposed reference/note limits are 64/500 characters with explicit validation; any longer existing UI requirement needs a bounded approved extension, never silent truncation.

### D08.9 GL reconciliation, classification and tenant safety

The GL is the monetary authority. Source documents establish obligation/origin/allocation facts; subledgers are recomputable scoped projections of the same posted lines and immutable mapping, never independently adjusted balance tables. At each tenant/as-of cutoff:

- `sum(all signed journal lines) = 0`; each journal balances, each source version/reversal is unique, and no zero/unsafe-integer line is accepted. Trial Balance debits equal credits and includes every supported control plus unresolved legacy codes.
- AR = sum CUSTOMER AR; CUSTOMER_ADVANCE credit = sum customer unapplied advances. AP credit = sum SUPPLIER bill/opening payables; SUPPLIER_ADVANCE debit = sum supplier advances. VENDOR_PAYABLE/VENDOR_ADVANCE reconcile separately to consignment sources. Gross obligations and advances are never collapsed by a signed role total.
- Each `LOAN:{id}` credit principal = its single linked agreement outstanding, or an explicit **unattributed legacy** amount; aggregate lender outstanding sums linked agreements plus that residual exactly once. BORROWER_LOAN_RECEIVABLE debit = sum borrower agreement principal; repayments/collections equal effective allocations and their control reductions. No negative outstanding is hidden with `abs`/`max(0)`.
- CAP credits = sum attributed owner capital; DRAW debits net returned drawings = sum owner drawing components. Unclassified OCA is a separate legacy reconciliation component, never counted again as new capital or guessed loan debt. Per-owner equity is contributions/drawings only, not ownership percentage of enterprise equity or retained profit.
- D07 EXPENSE_PAYABLE credit = unpaid expense source amounts net settlements/corrections. Selecting a payee or paying already-recognized cost creates no second payable/expense. Match loans/expense/trade settlement back to original source and money journal lines, with allocation totals checked on both sides.
- Every relevant financial-control line has one attribution or an explicit reviewed legacy residual. Money/OE counterpart lines are excluded; joins must not multiply a control line by number of payment legs or allocations. Cash/Bank/MFS totals select only liquid account families, never LOAN or borrower receivable.

Balance Sheet mapping: borrower principal and trade/supplier/vendor advances are assets; lender debt, trade payables, customer deposits and approved expense payables are liabilities; CAP less DRAW is equity, with OE/retained results and legacy classifications handled separately. Loan dueDate and terms support current/noncurrent analysis; do not hardcode all loans noncurrent, derive uncertain on-demand/maturity classification from approved terms, and do not fabricate a schedule. Interest income/expense affects P&L, principal/capital/drawings do not. Cash-flow evidence distinguishes ordinary trade/expenses (operating), borrowing/repayment and owner equity movements (financing), and lending/collections (normally investing for these non-financial retailers). Interest classification follows the applicable reporting policy and is not inferred from principal. Historical openings, allocations and legacy reclassifications are noncash and do not inflate today's flows. These are reporting acceptance requirements; Reports/Analytics implementation remains excluded and no existing backend full Trial Balance/Balance Sheet implementation is claimed.

Tenant/as-of/permission scope must be identical across reconciliation sides. A limited user receives only authorized subledger views, not a hidden full-control balance that reveals other relationships. Tenant-wide control reconciliation requires a separately authorized finance view. Preserve unreconciled counts/details only for permitted users; fail accounting acceptance on unexplained drift, do not auto-post balancing differences.
