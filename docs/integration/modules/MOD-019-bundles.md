## 5. Proposed architecture and unresolved approval

The frontend Product.kind Bundle is virtual. Do not create a fake stocked Set and then issue component stock separately. Quantity conversion and price of bundle parent are independent of component selling prices; changing a recipe never rewrites historical allocations.

No architecture in this packet is falsely labelled owner-approved. Owner decision references: D09.
