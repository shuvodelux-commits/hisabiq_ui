## Required scenario families for every affected workflow

For each connected action record: happy path; invalid/empty/too-long input and wire shape; no token/unverified session; forbidden role and least privilege; tenant A→B switch; stale principal/epoch; loading→ready→dirty→saving→saved/discard/error; 401/403/404/409/422/500; HTML/bad JSON/redirect; offline/abort/timeout; double click and lost response; concurrent change; idempotency/unknown outcome; server audit and rollback if a mutation is transactional; keyboard-only, touch, 320–1440 px and accessible name/status; Bangla/English and light/dark. A checkbox without an actual executable test and result is **not covered**.

For settings and logo writes, **journal balance, inventory stock movement, costing/rounding and split payments are N/A**: those operations do not post money or stock. **Cash-session policy is NOT N/A** to the cross-module `cash_session_enabled` toggle: its money movement effects must be verified under MOD-005 against the real backend, including opening-balance exemptions. Device-only offline POS conflicts are outside MOD-001 settings scope; tenant-local preference isolation is not.
