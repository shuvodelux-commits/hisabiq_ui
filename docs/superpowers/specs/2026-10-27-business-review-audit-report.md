# HisabIQ Monthly/Yearly Business Review — implementation specification
Date: 2026-10-27 · Status: user requested build · Parent: attached Reports UI/UX blueprint §11.
## Selected direction
Use one **Reports → Business Review** workspace, not new sidebar sections or a 12th Magic Patterns tab. Monthly, yearly, custom and last 24 completed months share one calculation engine. Label user-facing output **Business Review / Internal Audit Sheet**; never pretend it is a statutory audit, certified P&L, balance sheet or bank-approved document. Keep original eleven Reports destinations and existing 34 source reports.
## Sources and accounting rules
Consume `useReportData` / `buildReportData` as the sole operational preview source. Every visible figure links to existing `ReportDetails` evidence. Cash receipts/payment allocations are not sales or accounting profit. Recorded purchases are not cost of goods sold. Returns have no complete historical cost reversal; profit must remain unavailable where affected. Missing costs and unverified periods produce null/unavailable, never zero or today's replacement purchase price. Report past stock, receivables and payables as unavailable unless as-of reconstruction is proven; period-end snapshots cannot be summed. Keep signed transfer movement out of trading performance. No mutations, postings, account approvals or external transmissions.
## Owner journey
Reports header → Business Review → select monthly/yearly/custom/last 24 completed months → inspect concise Summary (sales/returns, expenses, purchases, receipt/payments, current stock/due, top 3 priorities) → Monthly figures (each row's source, missing-data status) → Documents (print short explicitly incomplete draft and CSV of observed monthly figures) → Review history (session-only drafts; don't advertise immutable external storage). Export explicitly states preview assumptions and reporting date.
## UI and QA
Use HisabIQ shared Button/Input/AppSelect/AppDateRangePicker/Table/StatusBadge, Geist/Hind Siliguri, 36px controls, compact 13/12/11 table roles, proportional digits, Party Ledger-style underline tabs. Bilingual labels and Bangla helper. Test desktop 1440, tablet 1024/768, phone 390/360, keyboard, dark/light, no global overflow. No new React Router route; `?workspace=business-review` persists URL state. Do not remove Magic Patterns visual authority or original report library filters and drill-downs.
## Deferred, not silently implemented
Historical balanced finance accounts, immutable issued audit archives, approved/reviewed signatures and RBAC, Premium entitlement checks, bank-specific pack composition and actual PDF/XLSX generators depend on complete source/settings/backend contracts (see attached blueprint R4–R7). Do not show controls for services that don't exist.
