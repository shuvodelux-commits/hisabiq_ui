## 1. Commercial principle

HisabIQ must be useful indefinitely before payment. Acquire the first 1,000 distinct verified business registrations, measure activation and retained use, and earn purchases through clear business value.

A Free business buying SMS or optional features is a valuable paying customer. Subscription conversion is not mandatory. Registrations, activated businesses, D30 retention, MAU, unique payers and subscribers must be reported separately.

The first 1,000 registrations are a funded validation milestone, not a profitability promise.

## 3. Permanent Free offering and trust boundaries

Free includes ordinary sales, purchases, returns, expenses, stock and party ledgers; cash/bank/MFS recording; compound units; split payments; basic owner profit, sales, due, expense and low-stock summaries; ordinary A4/thermal receipts, PDFs, statements, reprints and data export.

Do not charge per ordinary sale or print. Do not put security, tenant isolation, audit integrity, backups or financial correctness behind an add-on.

No commercial monthly transaction/customer cap in the pilot. Publish technical abuse safeguards and monitor real costs.

On downgrade, preserve records and existing products. Allow routine transactions with existing products even above the lower catalog cap; restrict new catalog expansion rather than making the shop read-only. Preserve historical settlement, correction, return, reprint and export rights. Existing attachments remain readable/downloadable; new uploads follow quota. Independently purchased passes and owned print packs survive unrelated subscription changes.

Start users on Free immediately. Reject an automatic full-suite 15/30-day countdown. Offer a contextual, optional seven-day advanced-report trial after useful data exists.

Reject a universal ৳149 monthly feature price. Test Reports 30-day access at ৳149 versus ৳199; ৳199 is the central baseline. Defer ৳49 purchases until automated payment and support costs are measured.

Launch SMS after reconciliation works, then an evidence-backed report pass. Storage follows secure uploads; print designs follow demonstrated demand. Avoid a crowded store or general-purpose credit currency.

Owner-only purchasing initially. Show price, term, expiry, inclusions and a clear decline choice. Manual renewal by default. Credit unused actual paid value of overlapping passes/subscription time on upgrade. Never double-charge for an included benefit.

Adopt the report's first-purchase refund, duplicate/failed fulfillment and unused annual-term policies. Consumed SMS remains subject to the explicit delivery/billing policy.

## 5. SMS

| Non-masking pack | Price |
|---|---:|
| 100 billable segments | ৳99 |
| 500 billable segments | ৳399 |
| 2,000 billable segments | ৳1,499 |

These replace the earlier ৳89/৳349/৳1,299 hypotheses. Publication requires effective landed supplier cost at most ৳0.40/segment and measured contribution of at least 30% of tax-exclusive revenue.

Obtain three written quotes and test two providers. Verify current contracting authority, reseller rights, taxes, MNP, validity, sender requirements, failure charging and delivery reconciliation. Do not treat advertised tariffs or delivery claims as contracted performance.

Credits have 12-month validity, visible expiry, reminders and carry-forward on recharge under published terms. Do not rely on expired credits for profit. No unlimited SMS/WhatsApp or monthly SMS allowance by tier; permit a controlled five-segment activation sample.

Bill provider segments, including Bangla/Unicode concatenation; show estimated recipient × segment cost before sending. Reserve, dispatch and reconcile credits with idempotency; no blind duplicate retries or credits from frontend success alone.

Keep credit liability distinct from earned revenue and wholesale costs. Authentication OTP is a platform cost. Masking is quote-only initially; it is not promised to every shop.

## 6. Support and Control Center

Free includes essential instructions, issue reporting, account-access assistance and product-fault resolution. Severity drives security/data-integrity response across all plans.

Initial support hours: Saturday–Thursday, 10:00–18:00 Bangladesh time, with published holidays and founder/rota coverage.

| Tier | Routine first-response target | Included scheduled help |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Two business days | Group orientation and one optional 10-minute activation callback |
| Counter | One business day | One 15-minute setup session once |
| Pro | One business day, priority queue | Setup plus one 15-minute configuration session/quarter |
| Plus | Four support hours | Setup plus one 15-minute configuration session/month |

These are pilot operational targets, not contractual resolution guarantees. No dedicated engineer or unlimited configuration at ৳599. Product bug fixes do not consume paid assistance allowances.

Reuse the existing SaaS shell, tickets, Sales column picker and React components. ByabsIQ remains the backend. Adapt NestSherERP's typed settings/dependency/audit patterns; never reuse its permissive entitlement stub as production gating.

Keep commercial entitlement, presentation settings, employee authorization and support delegation separate. Hiding Profit is a preference; denying employee access to profit requires server-side filtering across APIs, reports, details, exports and print.

Initial remote configuration uses exact, customer-approved setting commands: target business/user, key, value, before/after, revision, ticket and actual support actor. Consent is single-use and valid for 15 minutes; retries are idempotent. Customer sees history and Undo.

No unrestricted impersonation, copied sessions or requested passwords/OTPs/PINs. Staff identity, MFA, scoped access, revocation and audit are required. Live read-only support visits are deferred until repeated need justifies them.

Bound complimentary reports trials to seven days, once per business per 90 days, within an approved budget and audit. Refund/payment/ownership powers stay separate.

## 8. Gates and order of work

1. Verify the actual sale/purchase/return → stock → payment → party-ledger workflow, tenant isolation, error recovery and backup restoration.
2. Reconcile ByabsIQ's older PRO/PREMIUM trial/read-only policy with the permanent Free decision.
3. Approve implementation contracts separately for catalog, orders, settlement, grants, SMS ledger, expiry and refunds.
4. Integrate narrow consented support configuration and real supplier/payment services.
5. Validate actual report data and secure attachments before charging for them.
6. Begin the 90-day merchant experiment only after readiness.

Pilot gates: activation at least 50%; D30 retention at least 50% of activated mature cohorts; capacity below 75% planned support load; SMS margin at least 30%; credible retained-customer payback within six months; no unresolved critical money, stock, access or restore failures. These are management thresholds, not research findings.

Continue late-cohort D30 measurement through day 120. Pause acquisition on wrong-tenant access, unexplained ledger discrepancies, duplicate fulfillment or failed restoration. Validate demand and direct contribution before scaling beyond the first cohort.
